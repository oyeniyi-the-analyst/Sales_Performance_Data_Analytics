# Sales Analytics Project

This project focuses on understanding how a business generates revenue and where the strongest performance comes from.

Using structured sales data, I analysed product performance, regional trends, and revenue patterns to identify insights that can support better commercial decisions.


## Business Problem

Organisations often have large volumes of sales data but limited visibility into:

- Which products drive the most revenue  
- Which regions perform best  
- How sales trends evolve over time  

Without this clarity, it becomes difficult to optimise strategy and maximise revenue.


## Approach

I used SQL to explore and structure the data, focusing on answering key business questions around revenue, product performance, and regional contribution.

The cleaned dataset was then visualised in Power BI to create a clear and interactive dashboard.


## Key Insights

- A small number of products contribute a significant share of total revenue  
- Certain regions consistently outperform others in sales performance  
- Revenue trends vary across years, highlighting opportunities for growth
